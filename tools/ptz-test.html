Notes

Aspect ratio. On Windows the Frame Server hands other apps the format the first client negotiated. Open at 1920 × 1080 here and Teams inherits 16:9. Leave it on browser default and Teams inherits Chrome's 640 × 480.

Shutter. exposureTime is in 100 µs units, so 100 is 1/100 s. The equivalent fraction is shown next to the value.

Gated controls. Shutter, ISO, colour temperature and focus distance only bite in manual mode. Moving one switches its mode for you.

Missing controls. Chrome only exposes what the Media Capture spec defines. Gain, gamma, hue, backlight compensation and power line frequency have no web equivalent, so no browser can reach them on any camera.
