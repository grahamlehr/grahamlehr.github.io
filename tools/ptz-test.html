Log

Reading the result

OverconstrainedError means the camera has no UVC PTZ controls that Chrome can see.

NotReadableError means something else holds the device. That is the Teams contention case, and it is the usual failure on macOS.

NotAllowedError means the movement permission was declined. Reset it under the padlock in the address bar, in "use and move your camera".

Pan and tilt are usually arcseconds, so 36000 is 10 degrees. Zoom units are whatever the vendor felt like.
